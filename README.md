# TS Fonts desktop app

The TS Fonts app installs the fonts in your [TS Fonts membership](https://membership.tsfonts.com) sync list on your computer, keeps them up to date, and removes them when you disconnect or your membership ends. Fonts work in every design app: Adobe, Figma, Office and more.

## Download

- **Mac** (macOS 11 or later, Apple Silicon and Intel): [TSFonts-mac.dmg](https://github.com/tsfonts/tsfonts-agent-releases/releases/latest/download/TSFonts-mac.dmg)
- **Windows** (Windows 10 or later): [TSFonts-windows-setup.exe](https://github.com/tsfonts/tsfonts-agent-releases/releases/latest/download/TSFonts-windows-setup.exe)

You'll need an active membership to sign in. Get one at [membership.tsfonts.com](https://membership.tsfonts.com).

## Updates

The app updates itself. When a new version is ready, it shows **Restart to update**.

## Uninstall

Open the app → **Settings → Uninstall**. This removes your TS fonts and signs the computer out. On Windows you can also use Settings → Apps.
